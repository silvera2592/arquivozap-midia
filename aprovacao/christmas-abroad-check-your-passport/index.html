Spending Christmas or New Year in Spain, France or elsewhere in the Schengen area (most EU countries)? Before you book, look at two dates on your passport's photo page: • Date of issue: less than 10 years before the day you arrive • Expiry date: at least 3 months after the day you plan to leave The one that catches people out: if you renewed your passport before 1 October 2018, it may carry extra months from your old one. The date of issue can then be more than 10 years ago even though the passport hasn't expired, and that can make it invalid for entry to the Schengen area. Children's passports last 5 years, so check theirs too. Your airline or travel company can confirm what your trip needs. Renewing: • Online on gov.uk: £102 for an adult, £66.50 for a child (the paper form costs more) • You'll usually get it within 3 weeks of HM Passport Office receiving your documents, longer if it needs more information • Don't book travel until the new passport arrives: it won't have the same number as the old one Short on time? The 1 week Fast Track costs £192 for an adult and £156.50 for a child. The 1 day Online Premium costs £239.50 and is for adult renewals only. Neither works for a first adult passport, or from outside the UK. At the border, the EU's new Entry/Exit System (EES) may ask you for fingerprints and a photo. Children aged 11 or under only have their photo taken. There's no charge, and the record lasts 3 years. Keep a photo of each passport's photo page, everyone's issue and expiry dates, and the renewal reference in one place. Next time someone asks "is my passport still OK?", the answer takes seconds.
