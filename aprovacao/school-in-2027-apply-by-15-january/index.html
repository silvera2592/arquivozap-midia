Child born between 1 September 2022 and 31 August 2023? In England, they're due to start reception (the first year of primary school) in September 2027, and the application closes on 15 January 2027. The basics: • You apply to your own council, even for schools in another council area • List the schools in your real order of preference: you're offered the highest one on your list that has a place • List more than one: listing only one doesn't improve your chances • Each school has its own admission criteria (brothers or sisters already there, distance, faith), so read them before you rank • A place at the school's nursery doesn't carry on: you still apply • Offers go out on 16 April 2027. Late applications are dealt with after the on-time ones Proof of address: most councils want a council tax bill in a parent's name, a tenancy agreement or mortgage statement, or a recent utility bill or bank statement. Many ask for two or three documents, and some set an age limit, such as under 3 months for bills. Some also ask for the child's birth certificate or passport. Every council has its own list, so check yours before January. Moving house? Tell the admissions team straight away, and watch your council's change-of-address date. And don't use an address that isn't really yours. A place won with a false or misleading address can be withdrawn, even after your child has started. In Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland the process is different: check with your council or, in Northern Ireland, the Education Authority. Keep copies of everything you upload, the confirmation email and the reference number in one place. You'll need them again in April.
